In the first example, the segment $[4, 6]$ (the days with $9, 2, 2$) is sustainable: the balances after each day are $5, 3, 1$, and all of them are at least $-2$. The segment $[1, 3]$ is not sustainable: the balances are $2, -1, -4$, and $-4 < -2$. No segment of length $4$ or more is sustainable.

In the second example, the segment $[3, 6]$ (the days with $7, 0, 3, 5$) is sustainable: the balances are $3, -1, -2, -1$, all at least $-3$. Adding day $7$ gives balance $-4 < -3$. No segment of length $5$ or more is sustainable.

In the third example, already on day $1$ the balance becomes $1 - 5 = -4 < 0$, and every other day is similar, so there are no sustainable segments.
